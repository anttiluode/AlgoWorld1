Planet

heartbeat of the world

One zoom, five laws, a loop

Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to move, press Dive to fall. Planet, landscape, grove, bark, cell: each depth runs a different rule. Inside the cell's checkerboard is a planet again.

Each place hands four numbers to the place inside it. The heartbeat at the bottom is the clock and tide of the planet at the top. Switch a link off and that level gets neutral numbers instead, so you can see what the link was doing.
